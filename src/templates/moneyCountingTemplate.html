🪙 Meet the Coins

Penny

Value: 1 cent (1¢)

Color: Copper (brown)

Fun Fact: It takes 100 pennies to make $1.00!

Nickel

Value: 5 cents (5¢)

Color: Silver

Equal to: 5 pennies

Dime

Value: 10 cents (10¢)

Color: Silver (smallest coin!)

Equal to: 2 nickels or 10 pennies

Quarter

Value: 25 cents (25¢)

Color: Silver (bigger than nickel)

Equal to: 5 nickels or 25 pennies

🔢 Skip Counting with Coins

Skip counting helps us count money faster!

Count by 5s (Nickels)

5 → 10 → 15 → 20 → 25 → 30

Count by 10s (Dimes)

10 → 20 → 30 → 40 → 50 → 60

Count by 25s (Quarters)

25 → 50 → 75 → 100

💡 Money Counting Tips

Start with the Biggest Coins

Always count quarters first, then dimes, nickels, and finally pennies. This makes counting easier!

Group Same Coins Together

Put all your quarters in one pile, all dimes in another. This helps you skip count!

Practice Making Change

If something costs 60¢ and you have $1.00, count up: 60¢ → 75¢ (nickel) → $1.00 (quarter). That's 40¢ change!
